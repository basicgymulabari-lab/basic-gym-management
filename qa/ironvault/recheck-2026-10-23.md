## Limits and outstanding decision

All test writes use intercepted, synthetic Supabase workspaces. No production records, schema, auth settings, storage, or Supabase configuration were changed during this audit. Real production sign-in and data writes were not exercised.

Invoice deletion currently archives/removes the receipt only. It does not cancel money received, decrease revenue, or reopen member dues. Whether invoice deletion should also cancel its payment is an outstanding user decision. No financial semantics were changed in this recheck.

The existing unstaged whitespace change in auth-middleware.ts and pre-existing QA artifacts were preserved.
